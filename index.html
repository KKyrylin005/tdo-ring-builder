Secure payments, considered options

The Diamond Oasis provides encrypted online payment through our website, with Apple Pay and Google Pay available at checkout on supported devices. Choose the payment method that suits you and complete your purchase securely.

Custom designs: 50% now, 50% before shipping

For custom-design orders, The Diamond Oasis offers a 50% deposit to begin. Once your jewellery is crafted and ready to ship, we send photographs and videos of the finished piece for you to review. The remaining 50% is settled after you receive these and before dispatch.

Delivery & shipping

Standard engagement rings: estimated 7-10 calendar days to the UAE and 10-14 calendar days to all other supported destinations, including crafting and shipping. Product-specific lead times take precedence.

The published policy confirms complimentary shipping to UAE, KSA and Lebanon. For other destinations, confirm charges, duties and timing with our team.

Ring sizing & complimentary resizing

The Diamond Oasis offers one complimentary resize within the first 60 days after purchase, including complimentary shipping to us and back to you wherever shipping is required. Full eternity rings are excluded from this service.

Craftsmanship warranty & aftercare

If you notice a manufacturing concern, please contact us within 7 days of receipt. Following inspection and approval, we’ll arrange a repair or replacement and cover shipping both ways. Please keep your proof of purchase and the piece in its original, unaltered condition so we can assist.

Returns & made-to-order purchases

Enjoy complimentary returns and refunds within 3 days of arrival on eligible in-stock items only. Made-to-order pieces, natural diamonds and jewellery featuring natural diamonds, and all customised items, including engraving or resizing, are excluded. Our team is here to help you choose with confidence before ordering.

In-person & virtual consultations

Enjoy personal guidance from our jewellery specialists, in person by appointment or from the comfort of home. Explore diamond shapes, discuss your design and find the right fit. Contact us to arrange a convenient time and confirm your preferred location.
